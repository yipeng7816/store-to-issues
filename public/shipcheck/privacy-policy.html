ShipCheck Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24

Summary

  • No account or login server.
  • Apps, releases, and checklist data are stored locally on your device (SQLite).
  • No analytics SDK in v1.
  • Optional local notifications (Pro); notification content is not sent to us.

Data on your device

App names, version numbers, checklist state, language preference, and Pro unlock flag (Secure Store).

Data we do not collect

There is no backend in v1; we do not operate servers that collect personal data.

Third parties

Apple App Store and Google Play process Pro subscription payments. Checklist help links open Apple/Google documentation in your browser.
